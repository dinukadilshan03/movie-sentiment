# Conclusion

## Present These Points

- Audience reactions and early review behavior are meaningfully related to movie popularity
- The strongest consistent factor across the analysis is **early review volume**
- Audience score contributes positively, but sentiment works together with other factors
- The final predictive model explains about **46%** of unseen variation in `log_box_office`
- Limitations remain: heteroskedasticity, multicollinearity in the raw interaction form, and omitted factors such as marketing and franchise strength

## Closing Line

- Movie popularity is influenced by audience reaction, but the relationship is **multi-factor and conditional**, not a simple direct effect of positivity alone
